# Mitigating factors: what lawyers look for, and how the tool tags them

These are the project owner's research notes, kept here so the tags stay grounded in how these cases are argued.
Tags are auto-extracted from the decision text and may be wrong. The official decision governs.

## Key mitigating factors in Character of Discharge (COD) and Discharge Upgrade (DU) cases

The VA looks at the "totality of the circumstances," not just the unfavorable conduct on its own.

| Factor | Notes | Tool tag(s) |
|---|---|---|
| **PTSD** | Combat, training accidents or other trauma that changed behavior. "Liberal consideration" applies. | PTSD |
| **MST or personal assault** | Behavior changes, absences or insubordination after sexual trauma or physical assault in service | MST / sexual assault; Personal assault (victim) |
| **Depression, anxiety, mood disorders** | Documented clinical changes that caused a drop in duty performance | Depression / anxiety / other mental health |
| **TBI** | Undiagnosed concussions or blast exposure affecting impulse control and judgment | TBI (includes concussion, blast exposure, IED) |
| **Medication side effects** | Misconduct caused by prescribed military medications or treatment | Medication side effects |
| **Self-medicating** | Drug or alcohol use traced to coping with pain, TBI or PTSD | Self-medication (+ Marijuana / THC, Other drug use, Alcohol) |
| **Combat or overseas hardship** | Stressors of combat zones or austere environments | Combat / deployment |
| **Duress or coercion** | Unauthorized pressure or threats from peers or superiors | Duress / coercion |
| **Family obligations and emergencies** | Ailing spouse, child or parent; often context for AWOL | Family / personal hardship |
| **Age and immaturity** | Misconduct early in enlistment, when young and impulsive | Youth / immaturity |
| **Prior good conduct** | Medals, commendations and a clean record show the misconduct was an anomaly | Prior good conduct / awards; Length / quality of service; Prior honorable service |
| **Post-service conduct** | Weighted heavily for DU (less so for VA COD) | Post-service conduct / accomplishments; Character letters / references |

Plus, from the Army decisions themselves:
- **The board's answers to the four Kurta (liberal consideration) questions:** "Board found mitigating condition," "Condition outweighed discharge," "Condition did not outweigh discharge."
- **The board's reasoning:** "Found inequitable" or "Found improper."

## VA COD vs. DoD discharge upgrade

| | VA Character of Discharge (COD) | DoD Discharge Upgrade (DU) |
|---|---|---|
| Objective | Decides eligibility for VA benefits; does not change the DD-214 | Changes the character of service on the DD-214 |
| Legal standard | 38 CFR 3.12: statutory and regulatory bars | Equity or propriety (DRB, BCMR/BCNR) |
| Post-service conduct | Less weight; focus on in-service totality of circumstances | Heavily weighted |

When the BVA (VA) decisions are added, they'll need their own tags for the statutory and regulatory bars (38 CFR 3.12)
and the "insanity" exception.
